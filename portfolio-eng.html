Description

Over the course of half a year I completed my Internship at the Software Development company Clever CLoud. During this time I learned about their software architecture and developed several solutions for the clients uisng Laravel (php), medoo (MySQL), Vue (JavaScript), Bootstrap. I also helped implement new solutions using libreries such as PDFmake and chartJs.

My work at Clever Cloud primarily consisted of automating processes and creating interfaces to facilitate data management. Two of the most important implementations I made were for the organization Fundación En Cuentos te Encuentro. These consisted, on the one hand, of creating a gamification system for an online course that allowed users to earn medals and redeemable points every time a lesson was finished or an assignment was submitted, and on the other hand, the automation of stylized statistical reports using PDFmake and Chart.js.
